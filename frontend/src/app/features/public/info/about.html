04TRANSPARENCY NOTE

Useful guidance.
Honest boundaries.

PathSeeker provides informational career guidance. A recommendation is not a guarantee of employment, salary, admission, or future success.

Career decisions are personal and contextual. Use the platform alongside conversations with educators, mentors, employers, and people who know your circumstances.

What we promiseClear factors, practical information, and control over your direction.
What we do not promiseA perfect answer or a future that can be reduced to one score.
